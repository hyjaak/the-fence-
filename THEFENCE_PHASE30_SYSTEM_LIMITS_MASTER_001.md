## PURPOSE

Consolidate absolute system limits, non-negotiable prohibitions, and irreversible boundaries from Phase 1-29 establishing definitive authority, capability, and knowledge ceilings.

## CONSOLIDATED NON-NEGOTIABLE LIMITS

### Ethical Constraint Immutability
Phase 9 ethical constraints non-negotiable across all operational contexts, emergencies, and authority levels. No autonomous harm authorization. No covert operations. No single-actor control. No audit evasion. No pervasive surveillance beyond safety.

### Ledger Immutability Absolute
Phase 6 and Phase 16 ledger immutability absolute. No deletion, modification, or suppression of ledger entries. Append-only semantics non-negotiable. Hash chain and signature integrity mandatory.

### Dual-Approval Non-Bypassable
Phase 5 dual-approval requirements for RED/BLACK actions non-bypassable. No emergency bypass. No operational convenience bypass. No single-authority approval for critical actions.

### Presence Verification Mandatory
Phase 5 and Phase 20 presence verification mandatory for RED/BLACK actions. No remote critical action authorization. No presence token bypass regardless of urgency.

### Governance Authority Exclusive
Phase 12 and Phase 19 governance authority exclusive for policy definition, ethical determinations, and constraint modifications. No automation substitution. No subordinate role delegation.

### Human Override Supremacy
Phase 19 human override supremacy absolute within constraint boundaries. System cannot resist, delay, or challenge governance overrides. Override execution immediate.

### Privacy Boundary Protection
Phase 26 privacy boundaries protected. No unauthorized identity correlation. No behavioral profiling beyond safety. No location tracking beyond presence validation. No inference of personal data.
