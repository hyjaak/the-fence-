============================================================
SOVEREIGN RECOVERY CONSTRAINTS — NO UNDO ILLUSION
============================================================

TIMESTAMP (UTC)
---------------
Declared under sealed, idle, canonicalized, trust-minimized,
and failure-disciplined system state.

------------------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY OBJECTIVE
------------------
Define recovery as a **new, governed action**, never as a rewind,
undo, or erasure of history.

Recovery does not negate failure.
Recovery respects failure.

------------------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY PRINCIPLES
-------------------
- No rollback of truth
- No deletion of evidence
- No silent correction
- No automatic recovery

Recovery is intentional, bounded, and authorized.

------------------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY PRECONDITIONS
---------------------
Recovery is FORBIDDEN unless:
- Failure class is identified
- Failure record is sealed
- Authority explicitly approves recovery
- Scope and impact are declared

Missing any condition = DENIAL.

------------------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY CONSTRAINTS
--------------------
- Recovery occurs in a NEW execution context
- Prior state remains immutable
- Recovery actions are append-only
- Outcomes are independently auditable

------------------------------------------------------------

FORBIDDEN RECOVERY ACTIONS
-------------------------
- State rewinds
- History edits
- Evidence removal
- "Fix forward" without record
- Shadow recovery paths

------------------------------------------------------------

RECOVERY TERMINATION
--------------------
Recovery ends when:
- Declared scope is satisfied
- New state is sealed
- Audit confirms completeness

Recovery does NOT imply readiness for execution.

------------------------------------------------------------

ASSURANCE
---------
Recovery cannot rewrite reality.
Recovery cannot erase accountability.
Recovery cannot bypass canon.

------------------------------------------------------------

DECLARATION
-----------
THEFENCE recovers with restraint.
Nothing is undone.
Everything is accounted for.
