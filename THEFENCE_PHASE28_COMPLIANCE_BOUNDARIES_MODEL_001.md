# PHASE 28: COMPLIANCE BOUNDARIES MODEL
## Phase: Compliance Constraints
## Status: Defined

---

## PURPOSE

Define compliance boundaries separating internal constraint adherence from external regulatory requirements without self-certifying compliance or substituting system verification for legal validation.

---

## SCOPE

Compliance boundaries include internal constraint adherence per Phase 1-27, external regulatory requirements, legal mandates, organizational policies, and industry standards. Scope excludes legal interpretation and compliance certification which remain external professional responsibilities.

---

## COMPLIANCE BOUNDARY DEFINITIONS

### Internal Constraint Compliance Boundary
System operates within Phase 1-27 constraints. Internal compliance verified through ledger evidence, verification results, and governance validation. Internal compliance system responsibility.

### External Regulatory Compliance Boundary
System operates under external regulatory requirements. External compliance requires legal interpretation and professional validation. External compliance organizational responsibility beyond system authority.

### Governance Policy Compliance Boundary
System enforces governance-approved policies per Phase 12. Policy compliance verified through signature validation and enforcement logging. Policy compliance within system control when policies align with constraints.

### Ethical Constraint Compliance Boundary
System adheres to Phase 9 ethical limits. Ethical compliance non-negotiable and immutable. Ethical compliance verified through violation absence and governance review.

### Organizational Directive Compliance Boundary
System responds to organizational leadership directives per Phase 8. Directive compliance mediated through governance. Directive compliance limited by constraint preservation requirements.

### Legal Mandate Compliance Boundary
System accommodates legal mandates through governance mediation per Phase 10. Legal compliance may require constraint override with organizational approval. Legal compliance ultimately organizational responsibility.

---

## INTERNAL VS EXTERNAL COMPLIANCE SEPARATION

### Internal Compliance Verification
Internal constraint compliance verified through ledger audit, cryptographic verification, and governance review. Verification evidence-based using system-generated records.

### External Compliance Validation
External regulatory compliance validated by external legal and compliance professionals. Validation requires jurisdictional expertise and regulatory interpretation beyond system capability.

### Separation Rationale
Internal compliance mechanically verifiable through constraint enforcement evidence. External compliance requires legal judgment and contextual interpretation system cannot provide.

### Separation Maintenance
System provides evidence for external compliance validation but does not certify external compliance. Certification authority external to system.

### Cross-Boundary Evidence
System-generated ledger evidence used for external compliance validation. Evidence export follows Phase 16 audit requirements and Phase 10 disclosure boundaries.

### Boundary Violation Prevention
System does not claim external regulatory compliance. Compliance claims made by organizational legal authorities using system evidence.

---

## ALLOWED COMPLIANCE ASSERTIONS

### Internal Constraint Adherence Assertion
System asserts adherence to Phase 1-27 constraints based on ledger evidence and verification results. Assertion evidence-based and auditable.

### Policy Enforcement Assertion
System asserts governance policy enforcement based on signature verification and enforcement logging. Assertion verifiable through ledger reconstruction.

### Ethical Limit Preservation Assertion
System asserts Phase 9 ethical constraints preserved based on violation absence and governance confirmation. Assertion subject to external verification.

### Audit Trail Completeness Assertion
System asserts audit trail completeness within Phase 16 observability boundaries. Assertion acknowledges gaps per Phase 16 limitations.

### Constraint Violation Detection Assertion
System asserts detected constraint violations recorded and escalated. Assertion acknowledges undetected violations possible per Phase 15 adversarial limits.

### Evidence Availability Assertion
System asserts ledger evidence available for compliance validation. Assertion verifiable through evidence export and external validation.

---

## FORBIDDEN COMPLIANCE ASSERTIONS

### Forbidden: Regulatory Compliance Certification
System cannot certify compliance with external regulations. Regulatory compliance requires legal validation external to system authority.

### Forbidden: Legal Sufficiency Assertion
System cannot assert constraint enforcement legally sufficient for regulatory requirements. Legal sufficiency determination requires professional legal judgment.

### Forbidden: Industry Standard Conformance Claim
System cannot claim conformance to industry standards. Standard conformance requires external certification and validation.

### Forbidden: Jurisdictional Compliance Assertion
System cannot assert compliance with jurisdiction-specific requirements. Jurisdictional compliance requires local legal expertise.

### Forbidden: Perfect Constraint Enforcement Claim
System cannot claim perfect constraint enforcement without violations. Claim conflicts with Phase 15 adversarial limits and Phase 16 observability boundaries.

### Forbidden: External Audit Substitution Claim
System cannot claim internal verification substitutes for external independent audit. External audit provides independent validation beyond system self-assessment.

### Forbidden: Compliance Guarantee Assertion
System cannot guarantee future compliance. Compliance assertion applies to historical evidence only per Phase 13 assurance limits.

## HUMAN AUTHORITY IN COMPLIANCE DECISIONS

### Governance Compliance Interpretation Authority
Governance interprets compliance requirements and determines system response. Interpretation authority exclusive to governance not delegable to system.

### Legal Counsel Coordination Requirement
Compliance decisions affecting legal obligations require governance coordination with organizational legal counsel. Legal interpretation external to governance expertise.

### Organizational Leadership Override Authority
Organizational leadership possesses override authority for legal compliance requiring constraint modification. Override follows Phase 19 supremacy rules within ethical boundaries.

### Compliance Trade-Off Decision Authority
Governance decides compliance trade-offs between conflicting requirements. Trade-off decisions require human judgment considering context and priorities.

### External Validator Approval Authority
External compliance validators possess authority to approve or reject compliance claims. Validator authority independent from system and governance.

### Human Review for Compliance Assertions
Compliance assertions requiring external communication reviewed by governance before release. Automated compliance assertion prohibited.

## COMPLIANCE CONFLICT HANDLING RULES

### Internal Constraint Priority
Internal Phase 1-27 constraints take priority over operational preferences. Constraint preservation overrides convenience.

### Ethical Limit Absolute Priority
Phase 9 ethical constraints take absolute priority over all compliance requirements. Ethical limits non-negotiable per Phase 9.

### Legal Mandate Accommodation Process
Legal mandates requiring constraint modification escalate to organizational leadership through governance. Accommodation requires external organizational approval and Phase 17 change control.

### Regulatory Conflict Escalation
Regulatory requirements conflicting with constraints escalate to Tier 5 organizational leadership. Resolution requires external legal and compliance professional involvement.

### Compliance vs Privacy Conflict
Compliance requirements conflicting with Phase 26 privacy boundaries require governance resolution balancing legal obligation against privacy protection.

### Conflict Documentation Requirement
Compliance conflicts documented and recorded to ledger with conflict nature, resolution decision, and decision authority. Documentation preserves decision rationale.

### Conflict Resolution Recording
Conflict resolutions recorded to ledger with governance approval and organizational leadership confirmation when required. Resolution creates audit trail.

## REGULATORY PRESSURE CONTAINMENT

### External Pressure Recognition
Regulatory pressure including agency requests, audit demands, and compliance inquiries recognized and escalated to governance per Phase 10 external interaction boundaries.

### Pressure Mediation Through Governance
All regulatory pressure mediated through governance. Direct system response to external regulators prohibited. Governance coordinates organizational legal response.

### Unauthorized Access Denial
Regulatory requests for system access denied without governance approval. Access follows Phase 25 data access eligibility rules regardless of external pressure.

### Disclosure Boundary Enforcement
Regulatory disclosure requests follow Phase 10 disclosure boundaries. Governance approves disclosures with legal counsel coordination.

### Pressure-Induced Change Resistance
Regulatory pressure does not bypass Phase 17 change control. Changes follow governance approval process regardless of external urgency.

### Coercion Detection
Regulatory pressure suggesting coercion or improper influence detected and escalated per Phase 15. Detection triggers organizational leadership notification.

### Pressure Recording
All regulatory pressure events recorded to ledger with pressure source, request nature, governance response, and outcome. Recording preserves pressure history.

## EMERGENCY COMPLIANCE CONSTRAINTS

### Emergency Legal Mandate Handling
Emergency legal mandates escalate to Tier 5 organizational leadership immediately. Emergency does not bypass governance mediation.

### Emergency Compliance Change Process
Compliance-driven emergency changes follow Phase 17 emergency change constraints. Emergency does not eliminate approval requirements or validation gates.

### Emergency Disclosure Boundaries
Emergency legal disclosure requirements follow Phase 10 boundaries with governance approval. Emergency does not authorize autonomous disclosure.

### Emergency Constraint Preservation
Emergency compliance situations preserve Phase 9 ethical limits. Legal emergencies do not override ethical constraints.

### Emergency Audit Recording
Emergency compliance actions recorded to ledger with emergency justification, legal basis, governance approval, and organizational leadership confirmation.

### Post-Emergency Validation
Emergency compliance actions reviewed post-emergency for necessity and appropriateness. Review determines whether emergency justified actions taken.

## COMPLIANCE FAILURE ESCALATION

### Internal Constraint Violation Escalation
Internal constraint violations escalate per Phase 22 requirements. Escalation tier determined by violation severity and constraint class.

### External Compliance Failure Escalation
External regulatory compliance failures escalate to Tier 5 organizational leadership. Escalation includes legal counsel notification and regulatory authority coordination.

### Compliance Conflict Deadlock Escalation
Unresolvable compliance conflicts escalate to external organizational leadership for resolution. Deadlock acknowledgment prevents forced resolution.

### Compliance Investigation Escalation
Compliance violations requiring investigation escalate to governance. Investigation includes root cause determination and remediation planning per Phase 22.

### Regulatory Enforcement Action Escalation
Regulatory enforcement actions including notices, fines, or mandates escalate immediately to organizational leadership. Escalation enables organizational legal response.

### Escalation Recording
All compliance failure escalations recorded to ledger with failure classification, escalation path, and resolution status. Recording preserves compliance history.
