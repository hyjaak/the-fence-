EMERGENCY DEFINITION
--------------------
An emergency is a declared condition where:
- Normal execution is threatened
- Safety boundaries are at risk
- Immediate response is required

An emergency is NOT:
- Urgency
- Convenience
- Human stress
- External pressure

------------------------------------------------------------

AUTHORITY CONSTRAINTS
---------------------
- Emergency authority is predefined
- Scope is explicitly bounded
- Duration is time-limited
- Authority auto-expires

No permanent emergency authority exists.

------------------------------------------------------------

EMERGENCY ACTION RULES
----------------------

- No new execution paths may be introduced
- No doctrine may be bypassed
- All actions are logged immutably

------------------------------------------------------------

DECLARATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------

- Emergency condition must be declared
- Authority chain must be validated
- Scope and duration must be recorded

Undeclared emergency action is forbidden.

- Authority exceeds scope
- Duration exceeds limit
- Undocumented action occurs
- Emergency used to bypass governance
