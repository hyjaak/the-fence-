## ZONE A — TARGET LOCK
- Phase: Infrastructure
- Domain: High-voltage transmission lines in power grids

## ZONE B — PROBLEM SIGNAL
- What fails: Transmission lines sag or fracture under thermal expansion
- Why failure escalates into chaos: Undetected overheating leads to cascading power failures across regions
- Weak signals that appear before failure: Gradual temperature rises, current imbalances, weather correlation data
- Why humans miss these signals: Overload from manual monitoring and lack of real-time correlation
