============================================================
SOVEREIGN TRUST MINIMIZATION — ZERO ASSUMPTION MODEL
============================================================

TIMESTAMP (UTC)
---------------
Declared under sealed, idle, and canonicalized system state.

------------------------------------------------------------

TRUST OBJECTIVE
---------------
Ensure THEFENCE operates under the assumption that
no actor, component, environment, or signal is trusted
by default—including itself.

Trust must be earned, scoped, and revocable.

------------------------------------------------------------

TRUST PRINCIPLES
----------------
- Default deny
- Explicit verification
- Least privilege
- Continuous validation
- No implicit inheritance

------------------------------------------------------------

TRUST DOMAINS
-------------
- Human operators
- Automation agents
- External systems
- Internal modules
- Historical artifacts

All domains are isolated unless explicitly bridged.

------------------------------------------------------------

VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------
- Identity must be proven
- Authority must be validated
- Scope must be bounded
- Duration must be limited

Verification is mandatory at every boundary.

------------------------------------------------------------

TRUST DECAY
-----------
- Trust is time-bound
- Trust expires automatically
- Renewal requires re-verification
- Stale trust is treated as invalid

------------------------------------------------------------

BREACH CONDITIONS
-----------------
A trust breach is declared if:
- Implicit trust is assumed
- Scope expands without authority
- Verification is skipped
- Revocation fails

------------------------------------------------------------

BREACH RESPONSE
---------------
Upon breach:
1. Access is revoked
2. Execution is denied
3. Audit escalates
4. Canon remains authoritative

------------------------------------------------------------

ASSURANCE
---------
No trust is permanent.
No trust is global.
No trust is silent.

------------------------------------------------------------

DECLARATION
-----------
THEFENCE trusts nothing by default.
Control is preserved through verification.
